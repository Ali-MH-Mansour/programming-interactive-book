About this glossary

This glossary collects the English technical terms from every lecture of the programming course (L0–L18) and gives each one in four more languages, so you can follow the vocabulary whichever language you think in. Terms are grouped by the lecture where they first appear, and the last column tells you which section of the lecture uses the term.

The page works offline once it has opened — nothing is loaded from the internet. Choose a lecture and a language below and only those terms are shown, so you are never faced with the whole five-language table at once.
